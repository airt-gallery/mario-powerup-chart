Size, protection and throwing

One card per power-up: what it does, what happens if you get hit, where to find it, and one handy tip.

Colour keySize Protection and lives Throwing Flying Special moves

Grow-big mushroom

Size
In the games: Super Mushroom
What it does
  • Turns small you into big you.
  • Big enough to smash brick blocks from below.
If you get hit You shrink back to small.
Find it in Nearly every Super Mario game since Super Mario Bros. (1985)
Tip: Small with no power-up? One hit costs a life, so grab this first.

Tiny mushroom

Size
In the games: Mini Mushroom
What it does
  • Fit through tiny pipes and narrow gaps.
  • Jump higher, float down and run across water.
If you get hit One hit and you lose a life.
Find it in The New Super Mario Bros. games
Tip: A normal stomp won't beat most enemies. Use a ground-pound instead.

Giant mushroom

Size
In the games: Mega Mushroom
What it does
  • Grow giant for a few seconds.
  • Walk through enemies, blocks and even pipes.
If you get hit Enemies can't hurt you while you're giant, but pits still can.
Find it in New Super Mario Bros. (Nintendo DS)
Tip: Smash as much as you can before it wears off. It can earn extra lives.

Invincibility star

Protection and lives
In the games: Super Star
What it does
  • You flash and special music plays.
  • Run into enemies to knock them out.
If you get hit Enemies can't hurt you while it lasts, but pits still can.
Find it in Nearly every Super Mario game since Super Mario Bros. (1985)
Tip: It only lasts a few seconds, so keep running and don't stop.

Extra-life mushroom

Protection and lives
In the games: 1-Up Mushroom
What it does
  • Adds one life to your total.
  • It moves, so chase it before it falls off the screen.
If you get hit Nothing to lose: the extra life is yours to keep.
Find it in Nearly every Super Mario game since Super Mario Bros. (1985)
Tip: In most games, collecting 100 coins gives you an extra life too.

Fireball flower

Throwing
In the games: Fire Flower
What it does
  • Throw fireballs that bounce along the ground.
  • Beats most enemies from a safe distance.
If you get hit You lose the power and get smaller.
Find it in Nearly every Super Mario game since Super Mario Bros. (1985)
Tip: Some enemies, like ghosts and skeletons, shrug fireballs off.

Freeze flower

Throwing
In the games: Ice Flower
What it does
  • Freeze enemies into blocks of ice.
  • Stand on a frozen enemy, or pick it up and throw it.
If you get hit You lose the power and get smaller.
Find it in New Super Mario Bros. Wii and New Super Mario Bros. U
Tip: Freeze an enemy beside a high ledge and use it as a step.

Hammer-throw suit

Throwing
In the games: Hammer Suit
What it does
  • Throw hammers in a high arc.
  • Beats enemies that fireballs can't. Duck to block fire.
If you get hit You lose the power and get smaller.
Find it in Super Mario Bros. 3
Tip: It's rare, so save it for a tough castle level.

Boomerang flower

Throwing
In the games: Boomerang Flower
What it does
  • The boomerang flies out, then comes back to you.
  • It can grab coins and items on the way.
If you get hit You lose the power and get smaller.
Find it in Super Mario 3D Land and Super Mario 3D World
Tip: Throw it at coins you can't reach, then let it bring them back.

Flying and special moves

One card per power-up: what it does, what happens if you get hit, where to find it, and one handy tip.

Colour keySize Protection and lives Throwing Flying Special moves

Raccoon-tail leaf

Flying
In the games: Super Leaf
What it does
  • Spin your tail to hit enemies and blocks.
  • Run fast, then jump to fly for a short time.
If you get hit You lose the power and get smaller.
Find it in Super Mario Bros. 3 (later 3D games use a leaf for a similar tail)
Tip: Falling? Keep pressing jump and the tail slows you down.

Statue suit

Flying
In the games: Tanooki Suit
What it does
  • Does everything the tail leaf does.
  • Turn into a stone statue for a moment.
If you get hit As a statue, nothing can hurt you. Otherwise you lose the power and get smaller.
Find it in Super Mario Bros. 3
Tip: Turn into a statue when an enemy is about to bump into you.

Flying cape feather

Flying
In the games: Cape Feather
What it does
  • Get a running start, then take off and glide.
  • Spin your cape to knock enemies away.
If you get hit You lose the power and go back to small.
Find it in Super Mario World
Tip: While gliding, press back to swoop up and forward to dive.

Propeller mushroom

Flying
In the games: Propeller Mushroom
What it does
  • Spin straight up high, then float down slowly.
  • Press down while falling to drill downwards.
If you get hit You lose the power and get smaller.
Find it in New Super Mario Bros. Wii and New Super Mario Bros. U
Tip: Great for reaching coins and secrets high above the level.

Gliding acorn

Flying
In the games: Super Acorn
What it does
  • Glide slowly across long gaps.
  • Cling to walls for a moment; one extra lift in mid-air.
If you get hit You lose the power and get smaller.
Find it in New Super Mario Bros. U
Tip: Save the mid-air lift for the end of a long glide.

Frog swim suit

Special moves
In the games: Frog Suit
What it does
  • Swim fast, even against strong currents.
  • On land you can only hop.
If you get hit You lose the power and get smaller.
Find it in Super Mario Bros. 3
Tip: Brilliant in water levels, awkward everywhere else.

Penguin suit

Special moves
In the games: Penguin Suit
What it does
  • Belly-slide to knock enemies and break blocks.
  • Swim well, grip on ice and throw ice balls.
If you get hit You lose the power and get smaller.
Find it in New Super Mario Bros. Wii and New Super Mario Bros. U
Tip: The best choice for icy and snowy levels.

Cat bell

Special moves
In the games: Super Bell
What it does
  • Run straight up walls for a while.
  • Scratch enemies, or pounce down from the air.
If you get hit You lose the power and get smaller.
Find it in Super Mario 3D World
Tip: Climb up the side of the goal pole to grab it near the top.

Elephant fruit

Special moves
In the games: Elephant Fruit
What it does
  • Swing your trunk to knock enemies away and smash blocks.
  • Suck up water and spray it.
If you get hit You lose the power and get smaller.
Find it in Super Mario Bros. Wonder (2023)
Tip: Spray water on dry flowers along the way.

Notes and sources

Short notes that explain how power-ups work, how this sheet was made, and where the facts were checked.

Colour keySize Protection and lives Throwing Flying Special moves

1 Small, big, then powered up

Most games have three steps. You start small. A grow-big mushroom makes you big. A flower, leaf, suit or other power-up then gives you a special power on top.

2 What a hit does

In the very first game and in Super Mario World, a hit with a special power sends you straight back to small. In Super Mario Bros. 3 and the New Super Mario Bros. games, you usually drop to big first.

3 A spare in your pocket

Super Mario World keeps one spare power-up in a box at the top of the screen. It drops down when you need it. Some later games let you store power-ups too.

4 Each game is a bit different

The same power-up can work a little differently from game to game. For example, in Super Mario Galaxy the ice power lets you skate across water instead of throwing ice balls.

5 Pits still win

Even the invincibility star and the giant mushroom can't save you from falling into a pit or off the bottom of the screen.

6 About the names

The bold names on this sheet are plain descriptions we wrote. The small names are what the games call each power-up, so you can find them in a game or a guide.

7 Try this at home

Invent your own power-up. What does it look like? What does it let you do? Which colour group is it in? And what happens if you get hit?

8 Reading the colours

Green changes your size. Yellow keeps you safe or adds a life. Orange throws things. Blue helps you fly. Purple gives you special moves.
