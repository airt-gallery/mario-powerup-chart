# Super Mario power-up cheat sheet

A free, printable cheat sheet of 18 well-known Super Mario power-ups: 4 pages, A4 landscape. An at-a-glance table sorted into five colour groups (size, protection and lives, throwing, flying, special moves), a card for every power-up (what it does, what happens if you get hit, where to find it and a tip), and a notes page.

Built by MSTR BLDR on **AiRT Official**. An unofficial fan reference, written fresh in our own words.

## Pages

1. Power-ups at a glance: all 18 power-ups in one table, colour key, what a hit does
2. Size, protection and throwing (one card per power-up)
3. Flying and special moves (one card per power-up)
4. Notes and sources

## How to use

**View it free:** https://airt-gallery.github.io/mario-powerup-chart/

**Download the PDF:** [mario-powerup-chart.pdf](https://airt-gallery.github.io/mario-powerup-chart/mario-powerup-chart.pdf)

Or download the files from this repo and print the PDF at 100% on A4 landscape, in colour. You can also open `mario-powerup-chart.html` in a modern browser and print from there.

## Files

- `mario-powerup-chart.pdf`: the cheat sheet. Print at 100% on A4 landscape.
- `mario-powerup-chart.html`: the source. Chrome lays out the table and cards, checks nothing spills off a page, gets cut off or overlaps, and prints the PDF. The finished PDF has no scripts, links or form fields; fonts are embedded (Exo 2 and Barlow, both open-licence fonts).
- `index.html`: the landing page with a page-by-page preview (`pages/`).

## Checks

- Every power-up was checked by script against the Super Mario Wiki page for that item and its power-up form: the games named under "Find it in" and the key claim on each card (0 problems). The "what a hit does" notes were checked against the wiki's Small Mario page.
- Every page rendered and looked at; an automatic check finds anything that spills past the page edge, into the footer, is clipped or overlaps (0 found). The printed table and cards were read back and compared with the data (18 of 18).
- All wording is our own: no text from the games or guides, and no official character art, sprites, logos, item pictures or official fonts. The bold names are plain descriptions we wrote; the small names are what the games call each power-up. The colours are our own palette.

## The ASTARR prompt used

PROBLEM: When my kids play Super Mario, they keep grabbing power-ups and asking what each one does, and I can never remember them all.

ASTARR PROMPT

ACT AS: A long-time Super Mario player and information designer who makes clear, printable cheat sheets.

SPECIFIC TOPIC: A power-up cheat sheet for the Super Mario games, covering 18 well-known power-ups from the classic games to the newest, for a family playing together. Give each power-up a plain descriptive name, with the name used in the games in small print underneath. Write it all in your own words: no text from the games or guides, and no official character art, sprites, logos or official fonts.

ACTIONS: Make an at-a-glance table of all 18 power-ups, with what each one does in one line and which games it's in. Add a simple colour key that sorts them into five groups: size, protection and lives, throwing, flying and special moves. Add a card for each power-up: what it does, what happens if you get hit, where to find it and one handy tip. Add a notes page that explains how power-ups and getting hit work. Check every fact against trusted sources.

RESULT&RULES: I want a printable A4 landscape PDF of about 4 pages, with the colour key on every page. Easy to read, family-friendly, and marked as an unofficial fan reference.

ASTARR = Act As, Specific Topic, Actions, Result, Rules.
